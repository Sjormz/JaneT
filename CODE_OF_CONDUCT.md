# Code of Conduct

JaneT is a small project, and it should be a welcoming one. Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, discussions or reviews is expected to follow this code.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to open source.
- Assume good intent. Disagree about ideas, not people, and explain your reasoning.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful to the people reading it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning language or imagery of any kind.
- Sexualized content or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or encouraging others to do any of the above.

## Scope

This code applies in all JaneT project spaces on GitHub, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request, or contact the maintainer, [@Sjormz](https://github.com/Sjormz), on GitHub. Reports are handled privately. Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues: report those as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, and warn, temporarily restrict or permanently block anyone whose behavior breaks this code. The response depends on how serious the behavior is and whether it repeats.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
